# Buy all recommended products — October 14, 2026

One Buy now link on a confirmed, unexpired proposal opens Avery's Shopify cart with every recommended SKU. Shopify variant IDs are resolved against the current public catalog; quantities are whole cases, boxes or packs from the grouped purchase schedule, not the underlying number of pieces. The separate current store total makes changes from saved quote pricing visible. The link is also included in copied proposal text and remains clickable in a printed PDF.

Missing, ambiguous, unavailable or zero-priced variants, changed pack sizes, invalid quantities, expired/draft quotes and catalog outages disable the entire purchase link with an explanation. Products are never silently omitted. Existing source pricing, saved proposal totals and pricing mode are preserved. No schema change or new credentials are required.
